The World Is Our Playground

If the great outdoors is your playground… Then you are Tough Old Men. 

If you let your experiences instead of age challenge you… Then you are Tough Old Men. 

If you live life like a daily adventure… Then you are Tough Old Men.

Welcome to the brotherhood of Tough Old Men [TOM]. We are a group of seasoned men who can play just as hard as any kid. They may call us old, but we embrace it then show them how tough we are by the things we do. We are from all walks of life and our careers are in various fields. But our common love for the great outdoor brings us together in this league of Tough Old Men. We encourage each other to live an active lifestyle whether it be running, biking, hiking, camping, water sports, winter sports, team sports, extreme sports, mowing the lawn, or a little bit of everything. Get up, go out, and have an adventure today.
