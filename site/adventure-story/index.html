Our Adventure Story

Rob and Barron out for a casual Saturday ride.

The Tough Old Men adventure began with Barron Smith. A simple man who loved life and a good ol' adventure. He was the prime example of Tough Old Men [TOM]. Any chance he had, he was outside - wandering in the woods, running, hiking, kayaking, playing sports, biking, skiing, swimming and whatever else he could find to do outside. 

Barron was also an observer. As he observed, he was impressed with men in their 50s, 60s, 70s, and beyond who continued to actively enjoy life. These tough old guys were not spectators. They challenged themselves, competed against time. These men continued to actively work and play.

The idea of Tough Old Men came to Barron one day, around June 2009, while traveling between plumbing jobs (Barron was owner of a sole-proprietor plumbing business). He started thinking about the group of guys he cycled with on Saturday mornings. A majority of the guys at the time were in their 40s, all except Rob who was in his 50s. Even though Rob had hit the big 5-0, he kept up with the rest of the pack. And usually on the hill climbs, he would be one of the first to the top.

It impressed Barron, that Rob could hang with him and the "young bucks," taking turns pulling the pace-line, and completing century rides they raced in at events. Rob had also done some long distant riding across states with one of the younger guys in the group, Dave.

Then on a typical hot summer day in Northwest Georgia, Tough Old Men came to life. On this day the temperature approached 90 degrees and the humidity was about the same. By this time all the guys in the group were staring 50 years old square in the eyes if they hadn't already ran smack-dab into it. So it was a tough Saturday ride with lots of fast flat riding and long slow climbing. Barron and Rob had just crested a hill. Barron then turned to Rob and said, "Man, you're a TOM!" Rob had a blank look. He didn't know what Barron was talking about. Barron then explained, "You know, T-O-M. Tough Old Man!"  

From then on, TOM was used for compliments and encouragements. Barron and Rob kept talking and refining the idea of Tough Old Men. They wanted to use TOM as a way to motivate the older men to get outside, have an adventure, and of course, enjoy life. It is not about the age. It is about living while you can, being active, and having fun.
