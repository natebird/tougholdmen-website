Our adventure story

It started with a hill climb

One hot Saturday in Northwest Georgia, two cyclists crested a hill and a name was born.

The Tough Old Men adventure began with Barron Smith. A simple man who loved life and a good ol’ adventure. He was the prime example of Tough Old Men [TOM]. Any chance he had, he was outside — wandering in the woods, running, hiking, kayaking, playing sports, biking, skiing, swimming and whatever else he could find to do outside.

Barron was also an observer. As he observed, he was impressed with men in their 50s, 60s, 70s, and beyond who continued to actively enjoy life. These tough old guys were not spectators. They challenged themselves, competed against time. These men continued to actively work and play.

The idea came to Barron one day, around June 2009, while traveling between plumbing jobs. He started thinking about the group of guys he cycled with on Saturday mornings. A majority of them were in their 40s, all except Rob, who was in his 50s. Even though Rob had hit the big 5-0, he kept up with the rest of the pack — and usually on the hill climbs, he would be one of the first to the top.

“Man, you’re a TOM!”

On a typical hot summer day in Northwest Georgia, the temperature approached 90 degrees and the humidity was about the same. It was a tough Saturday ride with lots of fast flat riding and long slow climbing. Barron and Rob had just crested a hill when Barron turned and said, “Man, you’re a TOM!” Rob had a blank look. Barron explained: “You know, T-O-M. Tough Old Man!”

From then on, TOM was used for compliments and encouragements. Barron and Rob kept talking and refining the idea. They wanted to use TOM as a way to motivate older men to get outside, have an adventure, and of course, enjoy life. It is not about the age. It is about living while you can, being active, and having fun.
