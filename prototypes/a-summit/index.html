Welcome to the brotherhood

Get up, go out, have an adventure today

Tough Old Men [TOM] come from all walks of life and every kind of career. What brings us together is a common love for the great outdoors, and we encourage each other to live an active lifestyle.

It is not about the age. It is about living while you can, being active, and having fun.
